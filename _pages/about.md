## About Me

I am a theoretical and computational astrophysicist with more than a decade of research experience. My work combines analytical theory, numerical modelling, scientific software development, and high-performance computing to understand physical processes across a wide range of astrophysical systems.

I began my research in gravitation and cosmology, studying white dwarfs, quark stars, inflation, and extensions of general relativity. My work later expanded into computational plasma astrophysics, where I model the ionisation and multi-wavelength emission of stellar winds, shocks, and nebulae. Across these areas, I am motivated by the challenge of connecting fundamental physics with phenomena that can be observed and measured.

I also design scientific software for non-equilibrium plasma modelling and synthetic observations. I value reproducible research, careful numerical methods, clear scientific communication, and collaborative work across theory, computation, and observation.

## Professional History

### Production Operator, Syntro Health, Melbourne

*March 2025–present*

Working in the cGMP-compliant manufacture of clinical-trial pharmaceuticals, with an emphasis on precision processes, quality assurance, and regulatory compliance.

### Postdoctoral Researcher, Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies

*2022–2024*

Conducted research in computational and high-energy astrophysics, developing synthetic observations and numerical models of Wolf–Rayet nebulae, stellar-wind bow shocks, and their ionisation and emission properties.

### Doctoral Researcher, Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati

*2013–2021*

Investigated quantum-gravity corrections in white dwarfs, extended-gravity models of quark stars, and inflationary cosmology. This work led to a PhD in Astrophysics and Cosmology and was supported by Junior and Senior Research Fellowships from the Government of India.

My academic background also includes master’s degrees in Applied Physics and Physics (Astrophysics), and a bachelor’s degree in Physics, Mathematics, and Statistics.
