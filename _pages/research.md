My research spans computational astrophysics, compact objects in extended theories of gravity, and cosmology. I combine analytical methods, numerical modelling, and high-performance computing to connect fundamental physics with astrophysical observations.

## Computational Astrophysics

Numerical modelling of non-equilibrium astrophysical plasmas, radiation–magnetohydrodynamics, stellar winds, shocks, nebulae, and their multi-wavelength emission.

[Explore Computational Astrophysics](/research/computational-astrophysics/){: .btn .btn--primary}

## Compact Objects in Extended Gravity

The structure and stability of white dwarfs, neutron stars, and quark stars in general relativity, modified gravity, and models incorporating modified quantum physics.

[Explore Compact Objects in Extended Gravity](/research/compact-objects-extended-gravity/){: .btn .btn--primary}

## Dark Matter, Dark Energy and Cosmology

Theoretical studies of inflation, primordial reheating, dark matter, dark energy, scalar fields, and modified-gravity models of cosmic evolution.

[Explore Dark Matter, Dark Energy and Cosmology](/research/dark-matter-dark-energy-cosmology/){: .btn .btn--primary}
